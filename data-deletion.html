Data Deletion Instructions

Faceless Video System (working name).

The Faceless Video System runs on your own computer. It has no central server, so it holds no copy of your data anywhere else. Everything it stores about your connected accounts is in its local data folder on your computer.

1. Remove the software's access

  • Facebook and Instagram: on Facebook, go to Settings & privacy, then Settings, then Business Integrations. Find "Faceless Video System" and click Remove.
  • YouTube / Google: open myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose the app, and click Remove access.

Once access is removed, the software can no longer post to or read from your accounts.

2. Delete the local data

  • Delete the accounts folder inside the software's data folder. It holds the encrypted sign-ins.
  • Delete the analytics folder to remove stored statistics and comment excerpts.
