Privacy Policy

Faceless Video System (working name). Effective November 23, 2026.

The Faceless Video System is desktop software that creates short videos and publishes them to social media accounts that the user connects. This policy explains what data the software accesses, how it is used and stored, and how to remove access.

Use of YouTube API Services

This software uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the software accesses

When you connect a YouTube account, the software asks for these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish the videos the software produces to your channel, with their title, description and settings.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to confirm which channel is connected and to read your own videos' public statistics and comments.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read your own videos' performance, such as views and how long viewers watch.

The software cannot delete videos, change channel settings, or access any account you have not connected.

How the data is used

  • To upload videos to the channel you connected.
  • To compare your videos' performance and suggest improvements to future videos. For this analysis, video statistics and short excerpts of public viewer comments may be sent to the AI service the software uses (Anthropic's Claude API). They are not used for advertising.

Data from your account is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else except as needed to provide these features.

Where the data is stored

The software runs on your own computer. Your sign-in authorization is stored only on that computer, encrypted so that only your Windows user account can read it. Video statistics and upload records are stored in the software's local data folder on that computer. There is no central server that collects your account data.

Keeping and deleting data

Stored statistics are kept until you delete them from the software's data folder. You can remove the software's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or through Google's security settings page. After access is removed, the software can no longer upload or read anything from your account, and you can delete the local sign-in and statistics files.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For questions about this policy or your data, email nickcucchii@gmail.com.
